Government rebranding works

CLIENTMinistry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF)
SCOPERebranding and signage

Chromelite delivered rebranding works for MAAIF, applying the ministry's identity to signage and assets under a public procurement contract. Case study

Structural steel for hydropower

CLIENTInternational energy client, Burundi
SCOPEStructural steel supply

Chromelite supplied structural steel for a hydropower project in Burundi, covering steel bridge and powerhouse requirements. Beyond sourcing to specification, we prepared commercial invoices, packing lists, weight schedules and HS code documentation and coordinated transport from Uganda to Burundi.

  • Steel sections supplied to project specification
  • Commercial invoices and packing lists for cross-border delivery
  • Transport coordinated from Uganda to Burundi

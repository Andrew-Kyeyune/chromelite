What we offer

Data Analytics & Business Intelligence: our offerings.

Eight specialised offerings, delivered on their own or combined into a complete programme.

BI dashboards

Interactive Power BI, Looker Studio or Metabase dashboards connected to your live data, so managers see sales, costs and performance today rather than at month-end.

Data audit & cleaning

We consolidate scattered Excel files and exports, remove duplicates, fix inconsistencies and define clear rules, giving you one reliable source of truth.

Sales & customer analytics

Segmentation, branch and agent performance, product mix, churn and basket analysis that show which customers, products and locations drive profit.

Forecasting & prediction

Demand, cash-flow and loan-default models built in R and Python and explained in plain language, so you can plan stock, staffing and borrowing.

Monitoring & evaluation

Field data collection with KoboToolbox or ODK linked to indicator dashboards and donor-ready reports, without manual compilation.

Public sector reporting

Programme dashboards aligned to the Parish Development Model and NDP IV indicators for ministries and local governments.

Data warehousing

Central cloud data stores combining POS, accounting, EFRIS and mobile money data, structured for fast reporting with proper access controls.

Analytics training

Hands-on Excel, Power BI and R courses for corporate and public sector teams, using real Ugandan business data.

FAQs

Data Analytics & Business Intelligence: questions answered.

How long does a dashboard project take?

Typical projects run in four to eight weeks: mapping data sources, cleaning and connecting them, building a first version with your team, and refining it before handover.

Which tools do you use?

Mainly Microsoft Power BI, Looker Studio and Metabase for dashboards, with R and Python for forecasting and advanced analytics.

Do you offer Power BI training?

Yes. We run practical Excel, Power BI and R courses as public cohorts in Kampala, in-house sessions for organisations, or blended online and classroom programmes.

Can you help NGOs with monitoring and evaluation?

Yes. We set up field data collection with KoboToolbox or ODK and link it to indicator dashboards and donor-ready reports.

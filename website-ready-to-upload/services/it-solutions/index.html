What we offer

IT Solutions & Managed Services: our offerings.

Eight specialised offerings, delivered on their own or combined into a complete programme.

Custom business systems

Purpose-built software for the way you work: school management, SACCO loan tracking, inventory, point-of-sale, HR and payroll. Designed around your processes, cloud or on site, with staff training.

Websites & e-commerce

Fast, mobile-friendly corporate websites and online shops with MTN MoMo and Airtel Money payments built in. We handle domain, hosting, content and search basics so customers can find, trust and pay you.

Mobile applications

Android-first apps for field sales, customer loyalty, service requests and data collection, working offline where connectivity is patchy and syncing automatically when it returns.

Cloud & collaboration

Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace with professional email on your own domain, shared drives, video meetings and automatic backups, migrated without disrupting daily work.

Networks & security systems

Structured cabling, office LAN and Wi-Fi, CCTV, access control and solar-backed power for critical IT equipment, documented and labelled for easy future changes.

Managed IT support

A monthly retainer covering helpdesk, preventive maintenance, licensing, patching and quarterly health checks: the benefits of an IT department at a predictable cost.

Cybersecurity & data protection

Security audits, access policies, staff awareness training and backup testing, helping you meet your obligations under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

ERP, accounting & EFRIS

QuickBooks, Sage or Odoo set up and integrated with URA's EFRIS e-invoicing, so invoices, stock and tax records stay consistent and audit-ready without double entry.

In practice

Predictable IT support for one monthly fee

Each retainer begins with an audit of devices, licences, network and backups; management then receives a short quarterly report on system health, risks found and work completed.

FAQs

IT Solutions & Managed Services: questions answered.

Can you integrate our accounting system with URA EFRIS?

Yes. We set up QuickBooks, Sage or Odoo and integrate it with EFRIS e-invoicing so that invoices, stock and tax records stay consistent without double entry.

Do your websites accept mobile money payments?

Yes. Our e-commerce websites can accept MTN MoMo and Airtel Money payments, alongside card payments where required.

What does managed IT support include?

A monthly retainer covering helpdesk by phone, WhatsApp and email, scheduled on-site maintenance, licensing and patching, monthly backup checks and a quarterly IT health report.

Do you work outside Kampala?

Yes. We support organisations across Uganda and design systems for teams spread across several districts and branches.

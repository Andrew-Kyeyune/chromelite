What we offer

Branding, Signage & Fabrication: our offerings.

Eight specialised offerings, delivered on their own or combined into a complete programme.

Indoor & outdoor signage

Fascia, pylon, illuminated, directional and wayfinding signs built from durable materials suited to Uganda's sun and rain, with energy-efficient LED lighting.

Digital signage & LED

LED screens, digital menu boards and queue display systems with simple content management for remote updates.

Corporate rebranding rollouts

New identities applied consistently across branches, vehicles and assets nationwide, with site surveys, schedules and photographic completion reports.

Vehicle & wall branding

Fleet wraps, wall murals and trading-centre wall branding that turn vehicles and buildings into advertisements.

Kiosks, stands & pylons

Mobile money kiosks, exhibition stands and solar-lit signs, designed in 3D and fabricated in our workshop.

Structural steel supply

Universal beams, channels, angles, plates and fabricated sections for construction and energy projects, with invoices, packing lists and logistics handled.

Asset & branch mapping

GIS records of every sign, billboard and branch with photographs and condition data in a dashboard.

Project documentation

Progress photography, drone footage and detailed completion reports for clients, auditors and funders.

In practice

From 3D render to finished installation

After a site survey we produce a realistic 3D render for approval. The approved design is built in our workshop with quality checks on welding, finishing, wiring and colour, installed by trained crews on a scheduled date, then tested, photographed and handed over with a completion report and maintenance plan.

  • Site survey and 3D render for approval
  • In-house fabrication with stage-by-stage quality checks
  • Safe, scheduled installation by trained crews
  • Completion report and maintenance plan
  • Compliance with KCCA and district outdoor advertising rules

FAQs

Branding, Signage & Fabrication: questions answered.

Do you fabricate signage in-house?

Yes. Signs, kiosks, stands and pylons are built in our own workshop, giving tighter quality control and faster turnaround.

Can you handle a nationwide rebranding?

Yes. We survey each site, schedule installations across branches, and provide photographic completion reports for every location.

Do you supply structural steel?

Yes. We supply beams, channels, angles, plates and fabricated sections for construction and energy projects, including cross-border supply with full commercial documentation.

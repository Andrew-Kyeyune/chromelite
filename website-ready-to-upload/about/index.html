About us

Built in Uganda. Engineered for growth.

Chromelite Technologies Limited helps organisations build their systems, understand their data, design their identity and reach their markets, through one accountable team.

Our story

From signboards to systems.

We began in branding, signage and structural steel supply, delivering the physical face of brands for public and private clients across Uganda and the region. That work earned us a reputation for disciplined delivery: precise fabrication, safe installation, complete documentation and respect for deadlines, including on government contracts procured under the PPDA framework and cross-border supply projects.

Along the way, our clients began asking for more. They needed systems that work reliably, data they can trust, creative work that stands out and campaigns that bring customers through the door. So we added four further service lines: IT solutions, data analytics and business intelligence, multimedia design, and marketing strategy and campaigns.

We understand how business is done in East Africa: the centrality of mobile money, the demands of URA's EFRIS e-invoicing, the discipline of public procurement, and the realities of serving customers in Kampala and upcountry alike.

Leadership

Andrew Kyeyune, Director

Andrew brings over nine years of industry experience in digital, creative and technology roles, including Blu Flamingo Ltd and Neo Evolution Ltd, where he worked on projects for leading Ugandan brands in banking, beverages and telecommunications.

He holds a BSc in Applied Information Technology from ISBAT University and is completing an MSc in Information Technology. He is a Course Facilitator in the Faculty of Information and Communication Technology at ISBAT University, teaching software engineering, cloud computing, big data analytics and business intelligence, and is a member of the ICT Association of Uganda (ICTAU).

This combination of industry practice and academic depth shapes how Chromelite works: solutions grounded in sound engineering and data principles, yet practical, affordable and easy to use every day.

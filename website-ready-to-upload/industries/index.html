Industries we serve

Sector experience that speaks your language.

Each sector has its own regulations, customers and pressures. These are the challenges we solve most often, and how we combine our services to solve them.

Financial services & SACCOs

Challenge: Manual records and audit pressure

A secure loan management system replaces spreadsheets, with a portfolio-at-risk dashboard for management. Members receive SMS and WhatsApp updates, and branches get consistent branding that builds trust.

Schools & universities

Challenge: Fees tracking and enrolment

A school management system tracks fees, results and attendance, while online admissions and targeted enrolment campaigns fill places. Campus wayfinding signage helps parents and visitors.

Hospitals & clinics

Challenge: Patient records and visibility

A clinic management system organises records and billing, wayfinding signage reduces confusion, and local SEO helps patients find the facility.

Retail & hospitality

Challenge: Sales visibility and footfall

E-commerce with mobile money opens a new channel, POS analytics show which products and branches perform, and an eye-catching shopfront brings customers in.

Manufacturing & distribution

Challenge: Regional sales and EFRIS

ERP and EFRIS integration keeps invoices and stock aligned, distributor dashboards show sales by region, and new packaging and fleet branding lift locally made products.

Agriculture & cooperatives

Challenge: Collection data and payments

Member and collection systems record deliveries and payments accurately, while price and yield analytics guide decisions and farmer materials support training.

NGOs & development partners

Challenge: Donor reporting

Field data collection feeds indicator dashboards and donor-ready reports, while documentaries and IEC materials tell the programme story.

Ministries & local governments

Challenge: Reporting and public communication

Programme dashboards track delivery against national indicators, ICT supply is delivered under PPDA, and rebranding and sensitisation campaigns help institutions reach citizens.
